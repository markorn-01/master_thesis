# Pooled support for small inner-surface patches

CPU inspection of existing 64³ two-bubble catalogs at five adjacent transitions;
60 declared parameter/time comparisons. No hydrodynamic run or online change.

See the [full method, results and limitations](../../experiments/astrolink/PATCH_SUPPORT.md).

Main finding: at .13→.14, five small patches collectively support each larger
inner group. Unique coverage is 89.5% at the smallest displacement gate and
100% at wider gates in the illustrated setting. Overlapping support is counted
once. This repairs a geometric coverage rejection, not a proven physical merger.

The .12→.13 gap remains: earlier inner patches are below the unchanged
20-sample association cutoff. `raw_components.csv` retains their presence.

Start with `patch_sequence.png` and `pooled_support.png`. Detailed parent/child
decisions and supported child row IDs are retained alongside provenance and
verification records. Local row and group numbers are not persistent identities.
