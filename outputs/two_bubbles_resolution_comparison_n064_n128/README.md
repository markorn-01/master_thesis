# 64³ / 128³ two-bubble comparison

Imported GPU products at all 17 matching events pass local saved-product checks.
The prescribed source mass and mechanical-power budgets match. Full-state
online–offline equivalence was not repeated at 128³, and physical convergence
has not been established.

See the [findings, figure descriptions and limitations](../../experiments/wind_bubble/TWO_BUBBLE_RESOLUTION_RESULTS.md).

Start with:

- `event_0012_comparison.png`: two large inner components are clearer at 128³,
  while the 64³ inner samples remain fragmented.
- `inner_patch_sensitivity.png`: retained group counts still depend on radius
  and minimum sample count.
- `diagnostic_histories.png`: Mach histories become similar, but sampled density
  ratios remain substantially different.

Raw graph memberships, component sizes, scalar histories, verification records
and checksums are retained. Colors and local component IDs do not establish
physical shock identity or correspondence through time.
