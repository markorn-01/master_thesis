Figure~\ref{fig:online_surface_distributions} illustrates the
combined spatial and statistical view at $t=0.20$ for the
$64^3$ wind-bubble run. The corresponding animation advances
through 21 saved analysis events over $0 \leq t \leq 0.20$,
using fixed spatial bounds, camera orientation, color limits,
histogram bins, and count axes to support comparison over time.

\begin{figure}[htbp]
    \centering
    \includegraphics[width=\textwidth]{figures/preview_0020.png}
    \caption{Detected shock surfaces and distributions at
    $t=0.20$ in the $64^3$ single-wind-bubble simulation.
    Left: all 5128 selected surface samples outside the
    injection region, colored by the reconstructed Mach
    number. The points represent sampled surface positions,
    rather than a connected mesh; the star marks the wind
    source. Right: Mach-number and sampled density-ratio
    distributions for the same population, with one count
    per surface cell and no area weighting. Orange lines
    mark the medians, and the dotted line marks a density
    ratio of one. The displayed distributions describe the
    reconstructed samples and do not independently establish
    the accuracy of the sampled upstream and downstream states.}
    \label{fig:online_surface_distributions}
\end{figure}
