# Conditional inner families: spatial review

- `inner_families_points.png`: original refined points.
- `inner_families_oriented_disks.png`: display-only disks at those same points.
- `verification.json`: original coordinates, source/input hashes and settings.

Rows are 64³ and 128³. Columns show the earlier fragments and the next two
coherent observations; these are different time windows at the two resolutions.
Orange/blue indicate the two conditional support families. They are not proven
physical ancestry or source ownership. Black stars locate the wind sources.
Every sample in each plotted family is displayed, without subsampling.

Disk rendering is inspired by Pfister et al. (2000) surfels, not a reproduction
of their complete renderer. Disk radius is 0.45 native grid cells, with ten
polygon vertices per sample. Normals and positions remain unchanged; no
cross-sample connectivity, hole filling, or physical area calculation is added.
The disks make orientation visible but do not establish a smooth closed surface.
